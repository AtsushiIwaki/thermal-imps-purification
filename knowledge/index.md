# Knowledge index

These pages explain reusable contracts and how to assess evidence. The authoritative
[numerical conventions](../docs/numerical-conventions.md), [validation report](../docs/validation.md)
and [limitations](../docs/limitations.md) own conventions, measured results and their bounds.
No page below adds a numerical measurement or requires material outside this checkout.

| [Complex evolution](topics/complex-evolution.md) | Explain backend classification, conjugation and heat estimator rejection. | Supplying complex matrices or changing complex contractions. |
| [Checkpoints and RDMs](topics/checkpoints-rdm.md) | Distinguish exact storage, continuation and approximate physical reconstruction. | Saving, restarting or interpreting interval matrices. |
